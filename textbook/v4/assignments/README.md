## Shared AI Policy

Students may use AI to brainstorm, draft, check syntax, or compare alternatives unless a specific LMS setting says otherwise. Students remain responsible for the final explanation, verification, and correction.

Every assignment that uses AI should include a brief AI-use note:

- what AI helped with
- what the student checked personally
- what the student changed or rejected

## Shared Submission Rule

Assignments should be submitted as a single document unless the LMS assignment requests a separate diagram, SQL file, screenshot, or screen recording.

Classic Phase Workflow Stages Driving Question Modules
Requirements 1 · 2 What work does the organization perform, and what must it track to run that work reliably? 1, 3
Conceptual Design 3 · 4 What entities, attributes, and relationships represent the business — and does the ERD show that accurately? 3, 4, 5
Logical Design 5 How should the conceptual model be expressed as tables, keys, data types, and constraints ready for implementation? 4, 5
Implementation 6 · 7 Does the built schema match the approved design, and do the queries return what the business actually needs? 2, 6
Operation 8 Who has access to what, how are concurrent changes protected, and how is the data backed up and recoverable? 7, 8
Maintenance 9 When requirements change, can every artifact — ERD, DBDD, schema, queries — be revised consistently? 8

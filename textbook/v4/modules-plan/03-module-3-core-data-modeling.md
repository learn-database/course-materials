# Module 3: Core Data Modeling

### Purpose

- Build the conceptual modeling foundation students need before moving into deeper design logic and implementation-ready artifact production.
- Center the module on requirement interpretation, entity judgment, and critique of flawed conceptual models.

### Objectives

- identify entities, attributes, identifiers, and relationship types
- distinguish one-to-many from many-to-many relationships
- interpret simple Crow's Foot relationship patterns
- identify candidate entities and significant attributes from a business process
- create and critique a first conceptual ERD

### Human Must Know

- how to separate scope from background detail
- how to choose entities and relationships based on business meaning
- how to justify cardinality and optionality
- what belongs in a conceptual ERD

### AI May Assist With

- generating candidate entity and attribute lists
- producing first-pass ERD suggestions
- surfacing alternative model structures for comparison

### Christian Integration Focus

- frame requirements discovery as careful listening that helps keep people and organizational responsibilities visible
- connect accurate conceptual modeling to human dignity, responsible service, and truthful representation

### Integration Touchpoints

- include one critique prompt asking which stakeholders become invisible when requirements are read carelessly
- include one project checkpoint asking whether the proposed entities and relationships reflect the true work and people involved in the case

### Lessons

#### Lesson 3.1: Entities, Attributes, and Identifiers
- sort scenario elements into entities and attributes
- evaluate stronger and weaker identifier choices

#### Lesson 3.2: Relationships and Cardinality
- identify relationship types
- distinguish one-to-many and many-to-many patterns
- reason about optionality and participation

#### Lesson 3.3: Discovering Requirements and Drafting a Conceptual ERD
- extract requirements from a case
- draft and critique a conceptual ERD

### Assessment Strategy

Because AI can draft plausible ERDs, the module should grade critique, repair, and defense of conceptual choices rather than a polished diagram alone.

### Primary Graded Assessment

#### Model Critique and Defense

- Format: students receive a short business case and a flawed conceptual ERD
- Students identify errors, revise the model, and provide a short screencast or annotated explanation defending selected fixes

### Secondary Evidence

- timed concept check on entities, attributes, identifiers, and relationships
- short classification task on conceptual versus irrelevant case details

### What To Grade

- accuracy of entity and relationship identification
- quality of justification for identifier and cardinality choices
- ability to detect subtle modeling errors
- clarity about conceptual ERD boundaries
- ability to connect a modeling mistake to stakeholder harm, invisibility, or misrepresentation when appropriate

### Module Assessment Tasks

- identify at least five flaws in a conceptual model
- repair the model without drifting into implementation detail
- justify key modeling choices from the business case
- explain what information the case does not require the system to store

### Why This Assessment Holds Up Better

- it turns AI-generated diagrams into critique targets
- it rewards business-rule reasoning over diagram polish
- it requires explanation of why a fix is appropriate
